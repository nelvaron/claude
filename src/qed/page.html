Don’t Take My Word for It

November 2026

Abstract

Every sentence below does what it says. Choose one and it will prove itself on your screen, using its own letters, while you watch. Nothing here is a recording. Each proof is worked out on your device as it runs, and most of them answer to your hand.

About a minute and a half. Better with sound. 0 of 7 proved

  1. Proposition 1 (Ink). This sentence is written in wet ink, and it will smear wherever you drag.

  2. Proposition 2 (Exactness). This sentence contains exactly four a’s, four c’s, two d’s, twenty-five e’s, twelve f’s, one g, three h’s, twelve i’s, four l’s, fifteen n’s, nine o’s, six r’s, twenty-six s’s, fifteen t’s, five u’s, seven v’s, six w’s, five x’s, four y’s and one z.

  3. Proposition 3 (Chance). Dropped through a field of pegs, the letters of this sentence will always settle into a bell curve.

  4. Proposition 4 (Harmony). Every letter of this sentence is a note, and it harmonises itself as it plays.

  5. Proposition 5 (Translation). This sentence says the same thing in twenty languages.

  6. Proposition 6 (Flight). Give these letters three rules (stay close, keep apart, match your neighbours) and they will fly like a flock of starlings.

  7. Proposition 7 (Economy). This page is a single file: {{LINES}} lines of code, no libraries, no images, nothing downloaded. Here is every line.

Exercise. Write a sentence of your own, then choose how it should prove itself.

Appendix. The prompt

This page answers one prompt. Here it is, complete and unedited.

I need you to create the most impressive thing I can share with people when you launch. It needs to be embeddable in a post and i can preface it however you like. it needs to have a visceral impact on people to show what you can do and the range of capabilities. It should grab them quickly. Be amazingly clever about this.
